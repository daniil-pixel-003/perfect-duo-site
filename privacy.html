Privacy Policy

Perfect Duo is a private space for two people. What you put in it is shared with your partner and nobody else. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

In short

  • We keep only what you type or add: your first name, your lists, dates, memories and photos.
  • Only you and your partner can see your couple space.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app, or by emailing us.

Who we are

Perfect Duo is made by Daniil Kosiurka, an independent developer, who is responsible for your data under this policy. Contact: daniil.kosiurka@gmail.com.

What we store

AccountA random account ID created when you first open the app. We don't ask for your email or phone number.
Your couple spaceThe first names you each enter, the date you got together, and your 6-letter invite code.
Things you addIdeas, notes, links, shopping lists, tasks with their steps and due dates, special dates, "I'm in" hearts, and memories.
PhotosPhotos you choose to add to a memory. They're resized on your phone before upload. We only get the photos you pick, never the rest of your library.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, advertising ID or device fingerprints, and the app contains no advertising or analytics tools.

Why we store it

Only to run the app: to keep your couple space in sync between your two phones and to keep it after you reinstall. We rely on our contract with you (providing the app you asked for) as the legal basis.

Who can see it

  • Your partner. Everything in your couple space is shared with the one person who joined with your code. That's the point of the app.
  • Nobody else. Every request to our database is checked on the server, so other users can't read your data even if they tried.
  • Our hosting provider. Data is stored with Supabase, which runs the database and photo storage for us and may only use it to provide that service. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • The law. We would only disclose data if legally required to, and would tell you where we're allowed to.

How long we keep it

For as long as your couple space exists. If one of you leaves, your partner keeps the shared lists and memories. When the last person leaves, the couple space is deleted, along with its lists, memories and photos. Backups held by our hosting provider are deleted on their normal schedule, at most within a few weeks.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete any idea, task, date or memory photo in the app.
  • Leave and delete: in the app, tap your two avatars, then "Leave couple space".
  • Ask us: email daniil.kosiurka@gmail.com to get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. We reply within 30 days.
  • If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Perfect Duo is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has used the app, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced in the app.
