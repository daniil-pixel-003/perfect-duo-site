Common questions

How do I connect with my partner?
One of you taps "Start our space" and gets a 6-letter code. The other taps "I have a code from my partner" and types it in. You can find the code again any time by tapping the Invite button or your two avatars.

My partner's changes don't show up.
Check that both phones are online, then close and reopen the app. Changes normally appear within a second.

Who can see our photos?
Only the two of you. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete everything?
Tap your two avatars, then "Leave couple space". When the last person leaves, the space and everything in it is deleted.

Does it cost anything?
Perfect Duo is free to use. Duo+ is an optional subscription that adds every photo on every memory, "Just for me" tasks, as many lists as you like and every past answer. One subscription covers both of you.

How do I cancel Duo+?
On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Duo+ → Cancel. Or in Perfect Duo: tap your two avatars → Duo+ → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep Duo+ until the end of the time you've paid for.

I paid but my partner doesn't have Duo+.
Open Perfect Duo on the phone that paid, so it can tell your shared space, then reopen it on your partner's phone. Still not there? Tap your avatars → Duo+ → Restore purchases, or email us.

Can I get a refund?
Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
